HOW TO READ THIS

Assumptions and limitations

  • 01
    Patterns reduce the estimate

    Common passwords, keyboard/alphabetic/numeric sequences, repeated chunks, repeated characters, and year-like numbers receive penalties.

  • 02
    Character variety is only one signal

    The heuristic approximates guess difficulty from length and character categories, then adjusts for recognizable patterns. It is not a measured entropy calculation.

  • 03
    Ranges are deliberately coarse

    Displayed ranges approximate uncertainty around a median-like guess count. Actual password choice and attacker methods can change results by many orders of magnitude.
